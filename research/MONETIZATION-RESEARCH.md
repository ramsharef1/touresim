## 1. Affiliate Programs — Commission Rates & Fit

### Hotels / Accommodation

**Booking.com Affiliate Programme**
- Base commission: **4%** on confirmed accommodation stays
- Car rentals: 6% | Attractions: 4% | Flights: £2/€2 per booking
- Structure: Commission = % of Booking.com's take (not the full booking value)
- Cookie: Session-based (some networks offer 30-day via CJ/Awin)
- Performance tiers: rates increase with booking volume
- GCC relevance: **HIGH** — Booking.com holds 54–60% OTA market share in UAE/Saudi Arabia

Sources:
- [Booking.com Affiliate Support](https://affiliates.support.booking.com/kb/s/article/Commission-and-Payments)
- [Reacheffect: Booking.com affiliate review](https://reacheffect.com/blog/how-much-does-booking-com-pay-affiliates/)

### Tours & Activities

**GetYourGuide Affiliate**
- Base commission: **8%** | Negotiable up to 10–12% for high performers
- Cookie: 30 days
- Best fit: "Things to do" pages, itinerary pages, attraction pages

**Viator (TripAdvisor) Affiliate**
- Commission: **8%**, some sources cite 8–12% range
- Cookie: 30 days
- Best fit: POI pages, "best tours in [city]" pages

Sources:
- [GetYourGuide affiliate review](https://articlesbase.com/business/marketing-and-advertising/affiliate-marketing/getyourguide-affiliate-program-review-payments-and-earnings-in-2024/)
- [Viator affiliate review](https://www.creator-hero.com/blog/viator-affiliate-program-in-depth-review-pros-and-cons)

### eSIM (Fastest-Growing Travel Affiliate)

**Airalo Affiliate**
- Commission: **10%** per sale (flat rate)
- Payment via Impact (bank transfer or PayPal)
- Best fit: Visa guides, packing lists, "getting around" pages, airport guides
- Why it converts: travelers buy eSIM immediately before or on arrival; high-intent moment
- GCC relevance: HIGH — GCC travelers are tech-savvy, frequently international

Source: [Airalo Affiliate Program](https://www.airalo.com/blog/the-airalo-affiliate-program-everything-you-need-to-know)

### Travel Insurance

**SafetyWing / World Nomads**
- SafetyWing: ~10–15% commission on policy sales
- World Nomads: ~10% commission
- Best fit: visa guides ("do I need travel insurance?"), safety pages, budget pages

### Car Rental

**Rentalcars.com / Discover Cars**
- Typical commission: 40–70% of Rentalcars.com's margin (~5–8% of booking value net)
- Best fit: road trip guides, country getting-around pages

### Transfers

**Kiwitaxi / Welcome Pickups**
- Kiwitaxi: ~10% commission
- Best fit: airport arrival guides, "how to get from airport to city" pages

## 2. Display Advertising — RPM Benchmarks

### Network Requirements (2025–2026 updated)

| Network | Minimum Traffic | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Journey by Mediavine | 1,000 sessions/month | Entry level, lower RPMs |
| Mediavine Official | Income-based ($5,000/yr earned) | Premium tier, as of Jan 2026 |
| Raptive (formerly AdThrive) | 25,000 pageviews/month | Dropped from 100k in Oct 2025; 50% US/UK/CA/AU traffic required |
| Google AdSense | None | Entry point; lowest RPMs |

Sources:
- [Mediavine requirements](https://www.mediavine.com/mediavine-requirements/)
- [Raptive drops requirement to 25k](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/)

### RPM Benchmarks (Travel Niche)

| Network | Typical RPM Range | Peak (Q4) |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdSense (travel) | $3–8 | $8–15 |
| Ezoic (travel) | $8–15 | $15–25 |
| Journey by Mediavine | $10–20 | $20–35 |
| Mediavine Official | $20–44 average | $44–65+ |
| Raptive | $47–57 average (Oct–Dec 2025 data) | $65–100+ |

**Seasonal pattern:** Q4 (Oct–Dec) delivers 41–44% higher RPMs than Q1 (Jan–Mar). Travel RPMs peak before summer booking season (Mar–May) and holiday season.

Source: [Danny in the North: Mediavine vs Raptive comparison](https://danny-cph.com/mediavine-journey-first-3-months/)

### GCC Traffic CPM Premium

GCC visitors (Saudi, UAE, Kuwait) are among the **highest-value advertising audiences globally:**
- Saudi Arabia CPM: typically 3–5× higher than average emerging market CPMs
- UAE CPM: comparable to Western European rates
- This means Arabic-language pages targeting GCC audiences earn **premium RPMs even on AdSense**

## 4. Revenue Benchmarks at Scale

Real data from travel blogger income reports (2024):

- **Small (~15k sessions/mo):** $500–$2,000/month (mostly AdSense + low affiliate)
- **Mid (~50k sessions/mo):** $3,000–$8,000/month (Mediavine + selective affiliate)
- **Growing (~100k sessions/mo):** $8,000–$20,000/month
- **Established (~200k sessions/mo):** $15,000–$40,000/month
- **Large (500k sessions/mo):** $40,000–$100,000+/month

**Important caveat from 2024 income reports:** Several travel blogs saw 40–60% traffic drops from Google's August 2024 HCU update, with earnings falling proportionally. The sites that survived were those with genuine unique data and first-hand experience signals.

**Blended RPM (display + affiliate combined):** Top travel sites achieve $30–$60 blended RPM, meaning per 1,000 sessions they earn $30–$60. Sites with strong affiliate pages (hotel guides, tour pages) push toward $50–80 blended.

Sources:
- [Hannah on Horizon: $7k in 2024](https://hannahonhorizon.com/blogging-income-report-2024/)
- [Travel Blog Income Report Roundup](https://www.wanderherway.com/travel-blog-income-report-roundup/)

## 5. Arabic / GCC Monetization Specifics

### Booking Platform Affiliate for Arabic Audience

- **Booking.com Arabic affiliate:** Available, high conversion (Booking.com has 54–60% GCC OTA market share)
- **Almosafer affiliate program:** Exists but limited information publicly; worth direct outreach given their $270M GBV and Arabic-first audience
- **Wego affiliate/API:** Wego has a partner/affiliate program for metasearch; good for flight widget integration on country pages

### GCC Ad CPM Premium

GCC visitors are disproportionately high value for advertisers because:
- High disposable income
- High intent to purchase travel products
- Premium brands actively target GCC audience (luxury hotels, airlines, banking)
- Saudi Arabia and UAE CPMs on Google/Mediavine are among the highest non-English markets globally

**Practical implication:** Even at lower overall traffic, Arabic pages serving GCC visitors will earn more per session than generic global traffic.

## 6. Sponsored Content & B2B Revenue

Tourism board rates (industry estimates):
- Small destination feature: $500–$2,000 per article
- Country/major destination featured guide: $3,000–$10,000
- Annual destination partnership: $15,000–$50,000+
- National tourism board annual contracts (Visit Turkey, Visit Japan, Saudi Tourism): $50,000–$200,000+ for large publishers

**Arabic-first angle:** GCC national tourism authorities (Visit Saudi, Visit Abu Dhabi, Visit Dubai, Bahrain Tourism) have significant marketing budgets targeting their own citizens traveling abroad AND attracting international visitors. An Arabic travel platform is a natural sponsorship target.

## 7. Monetization Stack — Recommended Build Order

**Phase 1 (0–50k sessions): Affiliate-first**
1. Booking.com affiliate (immediate — add hotel deep links to every city/where-to-stay page)
2. GetYourGuide affiliate (add to things-to-do and POI pages)
3. Airalo affiliate (add to visa guides and packing lists — high conversion)
4. AdSense as baseline display (low RPM but no traffic threshold)

**Phase 2 (50k+ sessions): Premium display**
5. Apply to Journey by Mediavine (1k sessions now, but aim for Official at $5k/yr earned)
6. Raptive at 25k pageviews (note: requires 50% US/UK/CA/AU traffic — challenge for Arabic-first site)
7. Consider Mediavine Official once earning $5k/yr from ads

**Phase 3 (200k+ sessions): B2B + premium**
8. Direct tourism board sponsorships (Visit Turkey, Visit Japan first — top GCC destinations)
9. Almosafer / Wego affiliate integration (Arabic-specific hotel + flight widgets)
10. Travel insurance affiliate (SafetyWing/AXA) on safety + visa pages

**Note on Raptive geographic requirement:** Raptive requires 50% traffic from US/UK/CA/AU. An Arabic-first site will likely fail this unless English content scales. **Mediavine Official (income-based)** is the better premium network path for Touresim.
